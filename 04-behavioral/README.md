# Layer 4 — Behavioral / Staff Signals

Staff loops grade scope, ambiguity, and influence — not just execution. Build 8 STAR
stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result + what you'd do differently), each under 2.5
minutes spoken. Source material is already in Layer 1's project cards.

## Story bank (draft from these first)
- [ ] Ownership under ambiguity — v3 catalog / cross-system integration (nobody owned the seams)
- [ ] Technical disagreement — gate-state design vs catalog-as-truth (AISAGE-692-style debate)
- [ ] Migration without downtime — Visa EKS or Apple KServe dual-run
- [ ] Failure / incident — eval mis-scoring catch, or a production save with metrics
- [ ] Cross-team influence — getting another team to change their architecture (SpiceDB enforcement)
- [ ] Mentoring / raising the bar — team of 6 / team of 5 leadership moments
- [ ] Prioritization under pressure — Nov 10-style deadline triage
- [ ] A mistake you owned — pick a real one; Staff panels test this hardest
