# Layer 1 — Resume Depth & Project Readiness

Goal: no line on the resume is a liability. For each project: the 2-minute pitch, the
architecture you can draw, the hardest decision and what you traded away, the numbers
(and their derivation), what broke and what you changed, and the follow-up questions
a Staff-level interviewer asks when they smell bluffing.

## Files
- `skills-to-master.md` — the seven skill areas interview loops will probe, with depth targets
- `projects/` — one readiness card per core project

## Method (per card)
1. Read the card. 2. Explain it out loud for 10 minutes without looking.
3. Answer every "nasty question" on the card out loud. 4. Fix the card where you stumbled —
the card is wrong wherever your story is weak.
